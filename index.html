Envelope Check

Follow a fake email sent in your name.

See how your domain's public settings would treat a forged email, spot lookalike domains, and get a fix plan you can follow or pass to your IT provider. It's free, and no email is sent.

Use the part after the @ in your email address. We only read public DNS records.

What this checks: Public DNS settings for SPF, DKIM at common names, DMARC, MTA-STS, TLS reporting, DNSSEC and CAA, plus lookalike domain names if you ask. It doesn't scan your systems or test your mailboxes, passwords or Microsoft 365 settings, so a good result doesn't mean you're fully protected.

Privacy: We don't run a server or collect your results. Your fix progress and last result stay in this browser. Your browser asks Cloudflare DNS, or Google Public DNS as a backup, to look up the domain. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Check any changes with your IT provider before you make them. View the open-source code.
