Envelope Check

Can someone send email pretending to be you?

Check your organisation's public email security settings and get clear steps to fix what's missing. It's free, and you don't need a security team.

Use the part after the @ in your email address. We only read public DNS records.

What this checks: SPF, DKIM at common names, and DMARC. It doesn't test your mailboxes, passwords or Microsoft 365 settings, so a good result doesn't mean you're fully protected.

Privacy: We don't run a server or store your results. Your browser asks Cloudflare DNS, or Google Public DNS as a backup, to look up the domain. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Check any changes with your IT provider before you make them. View the open-source code.
